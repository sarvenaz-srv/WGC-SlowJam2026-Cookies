# Lixinho

[**Lixinho**](https://programminglounge.itch.io/lixinho) is a 2D platformer developed in **Godot** for the **Winnipeg
Game Collective's 2026 Slow Jam**.

## About the Game

Lixinho follows a small robot bug that has been swallowed by a monster.
Trapped inside the monster's body, the robot must climb its way upward
and find a way to escape.

The journey is not easy. Bile is rising from below, forcing the player
to keep moving, while different hazards throughout the monster's body
make the climb even more dangerous.

Along the way, the player can collect food to restore health and meet
NPCs who help the robot by granting new abilities. These abilities, such
as **double jump** and **dash**, allow the player to reach new areas and
continue the climb.

## Features

-   2D platforming and exploration
-   Rising bile that threatens the player from below
-   Environmental hazards
-   Health system
-   Food collectibles that restore health
-   NPC encounters and dialogue
-   Ability upgrades granted by NPCs
-   Double jump
-   Dash
-   Multiple areas within the monster's body
